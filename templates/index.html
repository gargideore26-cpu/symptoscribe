      ✦ How it works

      How does it work?

      SymptoScribe reads what the patient wrote using simple, explainable rules. It never names a disease.

      1. Clean the text

        The text is split into sentences and words, common spelling slips are fixed, and Hindi and Marathi words are matched to the same vocabulary as English.

      2. Find the symptoms

        Words and phrases are matched against a list of 40 symptoms written in English, Hinglish, Hindi and Marathi.

      3. Decide the status

        Negation rules tell whether each symptom is present, denied ("no fever"), unsure, from the past, or about someone else.

      4. Attach the details

        Since when, how severe, getting better or worse, where it hurts, and what triggers it. Vital signs such as blood pressure and pulse are read too.

      5. Check for red flags

        Simple rules, including age and pregnancy, raise a warning with advice when something should not wait.

      6. Write the note

        A template turns everything into the note for the doctor, with the red flags, vital signs and a table of symptoms.

      Why rules, and how well they work

      Every result can be traced to the words it came from, nothing needs training data, and the same text always gives the same answer.

      Example texts come from the public Symptom2Disease dataset on Kaggle.

      How does it work?

      From the patient's own words to a note the doctor can check.

      1. 01
        Patient's textTyped in, or sent by the patient
      2. Clean the textSplit into words and fix spelling
        02
      3. 03
        Find symptomsMatch against a list of 40
      4. Decide statusPresent, denied, unsure or past
        04
      5. 05
        Add detailsSince when, how bad, vital signs
      6. Check red flagsSimple rules, including age and pregnancy
        06
      7. 07
        Doctor's noteA short note the doctor can check
      Example

      “No fever, but a sore throat since Sunday.”

      Sore throat present, since SundayFever denied

      It supports the doctor and never diagnoses.
